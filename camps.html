Research notes

The search covered recreation centers, museums, community centers, theater, visual arts, dance, gymnastics, climbing, soccer, outdoor education and childcare programs. It began with local camp guides and search results, then checked providers’ own program pages and registration calendars. The scope is September 2026–May 2027, matching the school CSV. Dates were never inferred from a school’s closure calendar.

The resulting list is broad, but additional sessions may be announced later. Lakewood YMCA says 2026–27 Holiday Care registration opens September 14, 2026. Durham Arts Council winter registration opens October 2 and spring registration opens December 4. These are useful follow-up dates.

Source conflicts and choices
  • Museum: the first Rangers week starts September 28 in UltraCamp, while the information page says September 29. The registration listing is used. The October 5–9 week was waitlisted at the check.
  • Piedmont Wildlife Center: September 21 day camp and the September 21–25 week were waitlisted. Older pages show different prices; the current UltraCamp listings supply dates and fees.
  • Ninja: the September 28 title describes one day, but its date range ends September 29. That entry is omitted from date matches pending clarification. The other 25 dated sessions are included for age six.
  • JCC: September 30 was full with a waitlist in the booking portal. March 1 is preschool-only and is omitted. November 25 and April 21 have before-care only. The provider’s afternoon care starts 30 minutes after its stated camp end; confirm the handoff.
  • Walltown: the bundle text has inconsistent day counts. Only the $75 single-day price is shown.
  • Soccer and Boulder Garden: search snapshots were stale. Fresh provider and registration pages supply the fall dates in this directory; older prices and dates were discarded.
  • Schoolhouse: individual session pages identify Durham and the EE Pavilion entrance. Wake and Orange sessions are omitted. General “winter coming soon” text does not override the published Durham December 18 session.
  • Duke Faculty Club: the current Mini Camps page lists 13 dates for 2026–27; older search snapshots showed last year’s dates. Members and nonmembers in kindergarten–grade 6 can attend. Later spring dates have not been published. Dates come from Duke’s program page; CourtReserve availability could not be checked.

# Durham County kindergarten school-break camps, 2026–27

Research checked **September 12, 2026**. Scope: September 2026–May 2027, matching the existing school calendar.

**18 providers and leads were identified. Ten have dated sessions: 155 single-day sessions and 22 multi-day sessions.** Eight dated providers explicitly include kindergarten or age five. Durham Soccer Academy is a ninth option with a broad elementary-age description; confirm kindergarten placement. USA Ninja Challenge is the tenth and requires age six.

The interactive [directory](./camps.html) contains eligibility, locations, hours, costs, enrollment links, individual schedules, source conflicts and exclusions. The [calendar](./index.html) defaults to age five and single-day bookings.

## What the evidence means

- A published date establishes that a provider lists the session; it does not guarantee remaining seats.
- Waitlist/open labels are snapshots as of the check date, not live inventory. Registration links are outbound links; no registration or purchase was made.
- Multi-day sessions are stored once with their inclusive date range. The calendar displays them only when the user includes multi-day bookings, and labels the whole-session requirement.
- Undated, historical and restricted programs are not assigned to school closure dates.
- Grade-based eligibility is preserved in words; Minimum_Age=0 in the data means no separate numerical minimum was published, not suitability for infants.
- Durham is determined by the camp venue, not a provider’s mailing address or a Triangle-wide marketing description.

## Search method and limits

Searches combined Durham / Durham County / NC with teacher workday camp, schools out camp, school break, holiday care, track out and kindergarten, followed by provider-specific searches. Broad searches covered JCC/community centers, museum/science, municipal recreation, YMCA, theater, visual arts, dance, gymnastics, ninja, climbing, soccer, outdoor education and childcare. Local camp guides supplied leads; primary program pages and linked booking systems supplied the published facts.

Official Daxko, UltraCamp, Amilia, ActiveNet, Capitan, Studio Director, DanceStudio-Pro and iClassPro links were inspected or extracted from provider pages. Public browser rendering was used for the Ninja schedule. No private accounts were accessed and no provider was contacted. Some portals require an account, location selection, or additional navigation; a registration link alone does not certify inventory.

The search stopped after the major local program categories and recurring provider leads were checked; further hits largely repeated those providers, summer-only listings, prior years, older-child programs or venues outside the county. This is a broad researched directory, not a guarantee that every small or newly announced program has been found.
